Streamax eSIM/Data pools

One pool, shared by the whole fleet.

Per-SIM allowances strand data: one camera runs out while fifty sit half-used. Every Streamax data plan pools instead — the constraint is the fleet total, never the individual device.

Two ways to buy

Dynamic pool or static pool.

They behave identically day to day — devices share, nothing is capped individually. They differ in one thing: whether the pool follows your device count.

Default

Dynamic pool

You pick an allowance per device per month — 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 GB. The pool is that allowance times the number of devices, and it re-sizes itself every time a device joins.

Q = a × x
a = GB per device per month
x = devices on the account
Q = the shared monthly pool
  • Add 40 vehicles at 3 GB and the pool grows by 120 GB — no order, no re-quote.
  • A heavy device is balanced by the quiet ones, automatically.
  • One predictable number per device per month for budgeting.
Committed volume

Static pool

You buy a fixed block of data for the month — not per device, but for the account as a whole. The pool is whatever you bought, and it stays there however many devices you connect.

Q = d
d = GB per month, bought up front
x = devices sharing it
Q = the shared monthly pool
  • Known volume, known invoice, regardless of fleet changes.
  • Cost per device falls as more devices share the same block.
  • Adding devices does not add data — watch the headroom.

Worked example

3 GB per device, and the pool follows the fleet.

A dynamic pool at 3 GB per device per month. Nothing is re-quoted when the fleet changes — the pool simply is the allowance times the device count.

FleetArithmeticShared monthly pool
1 device1 × 3 GB3 GB
10 devices10 × 3 GB30 GB
11 devices+1 device = +3 GB33 GB
250 devices250 × 3 GB750 GB

Data is shared fleet-wide. No stranded per-SIM allowances, no manual top-ups — and a vehicle that uploads three collisions in a week does not have to be rescued by hand.

What happens over the line

Usage above the pool is not cut off — it is billed as overage at the destination's published overage rate — at or just above the in-pool rate, depending on the country. Alerts and caps in FT Cloud let you decide long before that matters.

Which one should you take?

Growing or uncertain fleet → dynamic; the pool keeps pace on its own. Stable fleet with a known appetite → static; you commit to the volume and your cost per device drops as you add vehicles to it.
